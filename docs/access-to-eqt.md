## 2.2. Access to EQT

## 2.2.1. Registration

Users can request access to the EQT by filling your details in the following form.

## 2.2.2. Domain applications

The EQT provides access to multiple domains, depending on the specific project and/or intended application.

Within the agriculture domain, the following projects are currently available:

ReGeNL – focused on regenerative agriculture (www.regenl.wser.wur.nl)

4Growth – focused on IT innovation in agriculture (www.4growth.wser.wur.nl)

For the consumer science domain, a single access domain is available:

CDP (www.cdp.wser.wur.nl)

## 2.2.3. Log in

Access to the EQT requires credentials. These credentials are provided after successful registration via the form mentioned earlier. Once you receive your username, you should set up your password as soon as possible. You can then log in the domain specific EQT website using your username and password.

WUR employees can use their WUR credentials to log into the EQT after requesting for access through the form in section 2.2.1.
