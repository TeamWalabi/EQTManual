    Background of the ReGeNL Easy Questionnaire Tool

    The agricultural sector, both in the Netherlands and internationally, faces the significant challenge of combining food production with nature restoration and sustainable earning capacity. ReGeNL project collaborates with a thousand Dutch farmers to develop and implement farm business models that facilitate this combination. Through intensive collaboration with partners across the food system, the transition to these new business models will yield a solid business case. This will ensure that profitable agriculture in harmony with nature becomes the new norm, ensuring that Dutch agriculture remains an international leader.

    Easy Questionnaire Tool (EQT) aids researchers by offering a tool to create and develop questionnaires to survey thousands of Dutch farmers interested in becoming part of regenerative agriculture. Scientific researchers bring together various agricultural concepts into the EQT in order to harmonize the agricultural data outputs and create comparable data across different studies. This will re-organize the agricultural science community to become more united, harmonized, and data driven.

    In support, EQT provides a central access point to all researchers in this project to harmonise, describe, and organise their data via the tool. The tool has integrated digital functions such as the library of harmonized measures. By making use of such tools, researchers will experience a reduction in the administrative workload associated with good data management practices while gathering higher quality data. Furthermore, digital services are available to support research processes such as the data management platform, ADAGIO which visualizes raw data into reliable insights, data validation, and scientific analysis. This will facilitate optimal virtual access to data and networks in the regenerative agricultural domain, thus bringing the agricultural community to its next level.

    The tool supports working according to the RRI principles (Responsible Research & Innovation) and FAIR data principles. FAIR is an acronym for Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable and means that the data is Findable and Accessible to all European researchers, both from academia and industry, Interoperable within the agricultural science domain and with other adjacent research infrastructures, and Re-usable as they can be aggregated and re-analysed across time (for longitudinal trend analysis) and across lands (for cross-land analysis) and ultimately across scale levels to allow higher level learning (advanced data analytics).
